Copy everything below into ChatGPT. Attach 3 or 4 of the example images (the doctor-explaining-a-model style) so it can match them.

You are making photo-style images for a medical tourism website. I need **one unique image for every treatment in the list below**.

**Style (match the attached examples):**
- A realistic, bright, clean photo.
- The organ or body part the treatment is for must be clearly in view: an anatomical model, a scan on a screen, the body part itself, or the medical device used.
- Ideally a doctor is explaining it to a patient in a clinic. Other scenes are fine if they clearly relate to that exact treatment.
- Landscape, 3:2, at least 1600 px wide.

**Do not make:**
- A generic group of doctors standing over a body in an operating room (only allowed for the "Common operations" category).
- Random people standing around in white coats.
- Biology-textbook drawings, diagrams or illustrations.
- Any text, logos, captions or hospital branding on the image.
- Two images that look alike. Every treatment gets its own distinct scene, model, doctor and patient.

**How to work:**
- Do one category at a time (the headings below), so the list is not too long for one reply.
- Name each file exactly like the treatment, for example `Knee replacement.jpg`.
- When all categories are done, put every image in **one zip file** and give it to me to download.
